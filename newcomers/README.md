# Newcomer path

Five short pages, to read in order. Together they take about an hour. You do not need to know OpenStack already,
and you do not need any particular deployment tool.

| Step | Page | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [The OpenStack services, for operators](openstack-services.md) | What each service does, what it depends on, what usually breaks |
| 2 | [Glossary](glossary.md) | Skim it once, then keep it open: the words of runbooks and discussions, one line each |
| 3 | [How to read a runbook](how-to-read-a-runbook.md) | The structure of every page, what the statuses and the ⚠️ / 🛑 warnings mean |
| 4 | [Reading path through the runbooks](reading-path.md) | The published runbooks in a sensible order, from understanding to incidents |
| 5 | [Your first contribution](first-contribution.md) | How to give back, starting with ten-minute contributions |

Then pick a task labelled **`good first contribution`** in any OzarLand repository. Each one says what to do, where,
and how you will know it is done.

Questions are welcome at every level. Ask in the **Q&A** discussions: "I don't understand why…" is a perfectly good
question, and the answer often becomes a page that helps the next newcomer. [Start a discussion](start-a-discussion.md)
shows how.

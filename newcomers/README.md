# New to OpenStack?

Welcome. This space is for people who are starting to operate OpenStack, or who want to understand what the
operators around them are talking about. You do not need to know OpenStack already, and you do not need any
particular deployment tool.

| Page | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| [The OpenStack services, for operators](openstack-services.md) | What each service does, what it depends on, and what usually breaks |
| [Glossary](glossary.md) | The words you will meet in runbooks and discussions, in one line each |
| [Reading path](reading-path.md) | An order in which to read the existing runbooks, from understanding to incidents |
| [Your first contribution](first-contribution.md) | How to contribute for the first time, step by step, including if you are a beginner |

Then pick a task labelled **`good first contribution`** in any OzarLand repository. Each one says what to do, where,
and how you will know it is done.

Questions are welcome at every level. Ask in the **Q&A** discussions: "I don't understand why…" is a perfectly good
question, and the answer often becomes a page that helps the next newcomer.
